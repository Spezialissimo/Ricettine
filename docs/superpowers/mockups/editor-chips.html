Recipe details: how the chips work

Chips you haven't filled in show as dashed "+ Prep time" placeholders, so you can see what's missing without a long form. Tapping a chip opens a small sheet from the bottom, within thumb reach. Tap outside or swipe it down to close. Changes save immediately; there's no OK button to hunt for.

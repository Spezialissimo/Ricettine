## Recipes

Most recipes are based on [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com/) meals, read through its public API, translated into Italian and adapted. No TheMealDB images are used.

- "Bigos, lo stufato del cacciatore" is adapted from TheMealDB's "Bigos (Hunters Stew)" and reworked to match the photo series below (vegetables, smoked pork, sauerkraut).
- "Insalata di ceci, coriandolo e formaggio di capra" is translated and adapted from the Wikibooks recipe [Kochbuch/ Kichererbsensalat](https://de.wikibooks.org/wiki/Kochbuch/_Kichererbsensalat) (German Wikibooks, by Ralf Roletschek and other contributors), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Translated and adapted, with different quantities.
- "Ravioli di ricotta e spinaci" and "Rotolo di pan di Spagna con crema al burro" were written for the demo to match their Commons photo series. The sponge roll quantities (4 eggs, 100 g icing sugar, 120 g flour, a quarter of a lemon's zest, 200 then 180 degrees C) follow the handwritten card photographed in the Commons series ("Step by step. 03 - Recipe") and the file description.

The CC BY-SA / Free Art License terms below apply to those texts and images only, not to the rest of the demo content or to the app.
